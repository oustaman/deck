Two factory campuses anyone can find their way around, delivered in half the time

Visitors, inspectors and officials kept getting lost. Architects and interior designers had tried signage before; none of it had stuck.

Client
A state-owned manufacturer
Region
Mainland China, 2018–2020
Scale
Two campuses of five to six buildings each
My role
Led the work through my consultancy

The situation

Staff moved between buildings by bicycle, scooter and golf cart. Workers, engineers, visitors, health inspectors and government officials all needed to find a place, a machine or a safe way out, and the numbering made sense only to the people who already knew it.

What I did

  1. Named places instead of numbering them. Areas took the names of cities, safety exits the names of classical poets: names people already carry in their heads.
  2. Put both languages on every sign, with arrows to the two nearest exits.
  3. Put every machine’s manuals, checklists and supervisors one tap away, in a simple bilingual web app, with directions between buildings.
  4. Proved it small, then scaled it. One floor in the first week, one building live in the second, a one-week pilot, then every required floor on both campuses.

What changed

  • 1 month, not 2delivered in half the agreed time
  • Found their way unaidedvisitors, inspectors and government officials, without an escort
  • Extendedthe client commissioned a follow-up project
